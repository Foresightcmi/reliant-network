Frequently Asked Questions: Senior Placement Advisory

How does The Reliant Network stay free for families?

Our advisory service is completely free to seniors and families. Communities pay a standard referral fee upon move-in, similar to how an executive recruiter or real estate agent is compensated by the property.

Can Medicare pay for assisted living or memory care?

Medicare does not cover long-term room, board, or custodial care in assisted living. However, Medicare continues to pay for 100% of doctor visits, physical therapy, and hospice care provided on-site.

How quickly can emergency placement occur?

In critical hospital discharge or sudden caregiver loss situations, our expedited team can arrange safety assessments and move-in within 24 to 48 hours.
