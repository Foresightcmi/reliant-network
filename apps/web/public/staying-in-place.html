The 4 Non-Negotiables for Aging in Place

Every contractor in our network is audited against strict NAHB and ADA Title III guidelines.

Bathroom Safety

Over 70% of senior home accidents occur in the bathroom. Curbless walk-in showers and structural grab bars eliminate high-risk step-over thresholds.

Stairway Accessibility

Multi-level homes present daily mobility obstacles. Dual rail motorized stairlifts and vertical platform lifts restore access to all bedrooms.

Zero-Step Ingress

Exterior stairs and high door thresholds trap seniors inside. Modular ramps and flush thresholds ensure unassisted entry and emergency exit routes.

Smart Safety Sensors

Non-intrusive radar fall detection, automatic night illumination, and smart emergency intercoms keep adult children informed without infringing on privacy.
