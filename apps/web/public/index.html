Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Procurement & Vetting Standards

Direct answers to commercial equipment logistics, insurance verification, permit requirements, and billing standards.

How does Reliant Verified screen and verify commercial fleets and contractors? ↓

Reliant Verified enforces a rigorous 3-stage credentialing standard: 1) Active state contractor license verification and good standing with the Secretary of State, 2) Comprehensive General Liability insurance ($1M–$5M per occurrence) with active Certificates of Insurance (COI) on file, and 3) Physical equipment depot verification ensuring genuine local staging, live maintenance logs, and verifiable on-time dispatch history. Operators who fail quarterly spot audits are removed immediately.

What roll-off dumpster size is recommended for commercial or residential projects? ↓

For residential room cleanouts or asphalt shingle tear-offs up to 25 squares, a 10-yard or 20-yard dumpster (typically accommodating 2 to 3 tons) is standard. For whole-home renovations, retail cleanouts, or light commercial construction, a 30-yard roll-off container (3.5 to 4 tons capacity) is ideal. For major commercial demolitions, multi-floor construction debris, and industrial fit-outs, a 40-yard roll-off container (5 to 6 tons included) provides maximum volume. Use our Dumpster Sizing Tool to calculate exact cubic yards.

What utility connections are needed for luxury restroom trailer delivery? ↓

Luxury restroom trailers require two basic on-site utilities within 50 to 100 feet: 1) One to three dedicated 20-amp, 110-volt electrical circuits (standard 3-prong household outlets or commercial shore power), and 2) A standard garden hose spigot delivering minimum 30–50 PSI water pressure. For remote off-grid locations, onboard whisper-quiet generators and fresh-water holding tanks with integrated pressure pumps can be reserved through our VIP Restroom Sizing Planner.

Can mobile cold storage containers maintain sub-zero freezing temperatures? ↓

Yes. Our commercial mobile refrigerated containers and walk-in cold storage units feature digital temperature control ranging from -10°F (-23°C) for deep frozen meats and pharmaceuticals up to 50°F (10°C) for fresh produce, floral staging, and beverages. Units are equipped with heavy-duty electric Carrier or Thermo King refrigeration units running on 208V/230V or 460V 3-phase power. Check specs with our Cold Storage Sizing Tool.

How does online booking and wholesale dispatch work on Reliant Verified? ↓

Clients enter project specifications into our interactive calculators to review transparent, upfront market rates. Once confirmed, payment is secured via Stripe escrow, and an automated dispatch payload is transmitted directly to the closest vetted local operator. The verified fleet fulfills delivery, staging, and pickup, while the client receives digital tracking and instant receipt documentation.

Do I need a municipal permit for dumpster or trailer placement on public streets? ↓

If equipment is placed entirely on private property (driveways, parking lots, private jobsite pads), no municipal street permit is required. If placed on a public street, alley, or sidewalk, most municipalities require a Right-of-Way (ROW) or Street Obstruction Permit with reflective safety barricades. Reliant Verified maintains detailed municipal permit guides across 257 US cities in our Municipal Permitting Network.

How do independent contractors claim their directory profile or territory monopoly? ↓

Licensed commercial contractors and equipment fleets can click 'Claim Profile' on their listing or visit the Fleet Operator Portal. By submitting business verification credentials, operators can unlock an exclusive city territory monopoly, receiving 100% of unbrokered direct commercial inbound quote requests for their designated metro.

What are the cancellation and tonnage overage policies for equipment rentals? ↓

Standard equipment rentals include generous base weight allowances and rental periods (typically 7–10 days for dumpsters, weekend blocks for restroom trailers, and 30-day billing cycles for cold storage). Excess weight is billed strictly per municipal certified scale tickets at transparent local landfill rates ($65–$95 per ton). Cancellations made at least 48 hours prior to scheduled dispatch are eligible for full refunds under our Official Refund Policy.
