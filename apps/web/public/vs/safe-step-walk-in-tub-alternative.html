The Practical Pitfalls of Walk-In Bathtubs for Aging Seniors

Television commercials make walk-in bathtubs look like tranquil, spa-like retreats. However, geriatric physical therapists and occupational health nurses frequently advise against them. To operate a walk-in tub, the bather must enter the dry tub, seal the watertight door, and sit shivering for 8 to 12 minutes while 60 to 80 gallons of water slowly fill the basin. After bathing, the bather must sit exposed again while all 80 gallons drain before the door latch can legally be opened. If an emergency occurs while the tub is full, emergency responders cannot open the door without flooding the home.
