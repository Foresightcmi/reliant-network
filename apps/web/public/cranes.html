Frequently Asked Questions: Heavy Mobile Crane Rentals

How is crane rental priced?

Crane rental is typically billed as an hourly portal-to-portal rate (minimum 4 to 8 hour guarantee) plus mobilization/demobilization and permit fees.

What happens if high winds delay the pick?

OSHA mandates lift cessation when wind gusts exceed manufacturer charts (usually 20–30 mph). Weather delays qualify for reschedule protections under our broker terms.

How does the 15% escrow deposit work?

Your 15% deposit locks in the crane mobilization on the carrier's schedule. The funds are held in escrow and credited toward the total billing upon lift completion.
